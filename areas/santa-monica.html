Repair or replacement should follow the findings.

An older water heater in a Santa Monica home is not judged by its label alone. The actual fault, tank condition, available parts and household needs belong in the decision. Separate the equipment choice from the installation work.

A replacement involves access, removal and appropriate connections as well as the new equipment. Changing heater type may add requirements that a like-for-like swap does not have. Ask what a repair addresses and what a replacement proposal includes. Keep warranty or service records if you have them, without assuming coverage. For a property with shared access or an owner approval process, include those arrangements before scheduling is confirmed.

Questions for your Santa Monica home

Does a puddle prove that the tank wall failed?

Not by itself. A nearby connection or another source may be involved and needs identification.

Is advertised equipment pricing the installed cost?

Do not assume so. Access, connections and the agreed work must be part of the comparison.
