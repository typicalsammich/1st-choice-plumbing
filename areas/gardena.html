Plumbing in Gardena, CA

A recurring clog deserves more than another quick fix.

If your Gardena kitchen or bathroom drain clears for a day and then slows again, that history is worth sharing. A temporary improvement does not tell you where the remaining restriction is. Note which drains are affected and how long each clearing helped. Avoid adding more drain chemicals, especially when water is standing, and tell the plumber about products already used.

Questions for your Gardena home

Should I use a stronger cleaner each time?

No. Mixing or repeatedly adding chemicals can create hazards without resolving the underlying restriction.

Is every repeat clog in the main sewer?

No. A local branch or fixture can be involved. Multiple affected drains change what needs checking.
