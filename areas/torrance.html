A new water-heater sound is information, not a verdict.

A popping or rumbling sound from your Torrance water heater can prompt a worry about immediate replacement. The noise alone does not make that decision. Note when it begins and whether hot water, recovery time or visible moisture changed too. Read the model only if it is safe to reach. Leave burner, gas and electrical components to an appropriate professional.

Bring the timing and equipment details together.

Sounds during heating, after water use and when a faucet opens are not necessarily the same issue. A brief recording from a safe distance can help explain what you hear. Do not improvise a tank flush or repeatedly reset controls to test the noise. If there is gas odor, leave and contact the utility or emergency services from a safe location. Questions for your Torrance home

Is every popping sound sediment?

No. Deposits are one possibility; the heater type and other observations matter.

Should I raise the temperature to stop noise?

That is not a noise remedy and may increase scalding risk. Follow the unit’s instructions.
