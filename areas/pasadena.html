Plumbing in Pasadena, CA

A newer fixture can still depend on older connections.

When only part of a Pasadena home has been updated, the visible fixture may not tell you much about the plumbing connected to it. Mention any previous alterations you know about and where a recurring symptom appears. Avoid assuming the newest part is responsible or that the oldest connection must be replaced. The useful question is how the actual installation is behaving.

History is useful context, not a substitute for checking.

A faucet drip, weak shower and slow drain can have unrelated causes even in the same room. Explain each symptom rather than combining them into one guess. Keep records of earlier repairs if available, including product models or parts changed. For a planned improvement, show what was retained and what you hope to update next. This can guide a discussion of repairs or new connections without inventing conclusions about hidden piping.
