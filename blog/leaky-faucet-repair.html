When to Replace a Leaky Faucet

A small drip can become a costly nuisance when left unattended.

What homeowners should know

Worn cartridges, seals and connections are common causes. Sometimes a repair is enough; older fixtures with corrosion or recurring leaks may be better candidates for replacement.
